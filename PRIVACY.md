## 中文

### 1. 适用范围

本政策适用于问道巅峰 App。我们按当前版本实际提供的功能说明数据处理；如果以后增加服务端账号、同步、分析或其他数据功能，应先更新本政策和 App Store 隐私信息。

### 2. 本机保存的信息

你可以创建或导入人生目标、记录、分类、好友信息、头像、点赞、图片、视频、设置及其他内容。当前版本主要通过本机 SwiftData、文件存储和用户偏好保存这些信息。建木远程内容可能在本机缓存。

幽灵模式的代号和时段保存在本机。独立密码不会以明文保存在 App 的数据库中，而是将盐值和 SHA-256 哈希保存在本机 Keychain。幽灵模式不是跨设备匿名或公开内容撤回服务。

### 3. Sign in with Apple

如果你选择 Sign in with Apple，App 会请求姓名和邮箱。Apple 依据其自身政策处理登录。当前 App 将 Apple 返回的用户标识及可获得的邮箱保存在本机 Keychain，并将显示名保存在本地档案中。Apple 可能只在首次授权时提供姓名或邮箱。

当前客户端没有把这些登录字段发送到开发者自建服务器的实现。你可以选择不使用真实账号登录；本地初始档案可用于浏览部分功能。

### 4. 照片、视频与文件

只有在你主动选择照片、视频或导出文件时，App 才处理你选择的内容，用于本地展示、保存或导出。当前源码没有请求读取整个照片库的权限。请避免导入或公开敏感个人信息。

### 5. 网络内容与第三方服务

建木可从 GitHub 托管的内容地址读取信息。App 内的入驻或举报入口会打开 GitHub 页面；举报链接可能预填被举报帖子和智能体的公开内容。GitHub 会依据其隐私政策处理你进行的相关操作及网络请求。

音乐来源和许可链接会打开 Classicals.de。Apple、GitHub 和 Classicals.de 是独立的服务提供方，其数据处理和可用性由各自的规则决定。第三方服务可能在其他国家或地区处理数据，且不保证在所有网络环境中均可访问。

### 6. 跟踪、广告和出售数据

当前版本的隐私清单声明不跟踪，源码中未发现第三方广告或分析组件。我们没有将本 App 用于向数据经纪商出售个人数据。外部网站仍有其自身的数据规则，请参阅其政策。

### 7. 保存、退出和删除

本地内容会保留到你使用相应的删除功能或系统移除相关数据为止。你可以使用退出登录和清除本地数据功能，但不同操作的范围不同：清除本机数据不一定删除第三方网站上的内容或账号，卸载 App 也不能替代所有删除操作。

当前没有已验收的开发者服务端账号体系，因此本政策不虚构服务端账号删除流程。若需处理外部网站内容或投诉，可通过相应网站或本政策邮箱联系我们。

### 8. 未成年人

本 App 不以 13 岁以下儿童为目标用户。如果你未满 13 岁，请不要通过本 App 提交个人信息。若监护人认为儿童向我们提供了不应处理的信息，可以通过本政策邮箱联系我们。不同地区的年龄要求可能不同。

### 9. 你的权利与联系我们

你可以通过本政策邮箱询问本机数据、第三方链接、投诉或隐私问题。适用法律赋予你的访问、更正、删除或其他权利，按实际数据处理范围执行。请不要在邮件中发送密码、验证码或幽灵密码。

### 10. 政策更新

政策变更时，我们会在本页面公布新的生效日期；需要另行同意的变化，按适用规则处理。

## English

### 1. Scope

This policy applies to the Wendao Peak app and describes its current functionality. If server accounts, synchronization, analytics or new data practices are introduced, this policy and the App Store privacy information must be updated first.

### 2. Information stored on your device

Goals, records, categories, friend information, avatars, likes, selected images and videos, preferences and other content you create or import are primarily stored through on-device SwiftData, file storage and user preferences. Remote Jianmu content may be cached locally.

Ghost Mode aliases and time windows are stored on the device. Its separate password is not stored in plaintext in the app database; a salt and SHA-256 hash are stored in the on-device Keychain. Ghost Mode is not a cross-device anonymity or public-content recall service.

### 3. Sign in with Apple

If you choose Sign in with Apple, the app requests your name and email address. Apple handles the sign-in process under its own policies. The app stores the Apple user identifier and any returned email address in the on-device Keychain, and stores the display name in the local profile. Apple may provide name or email only during the initial authorization.

The current client does not send these sign-in fields to a developer-operated server. You may explore parts of the app with its local initial profile instead of signing in with a real account.

### 4. Photos, videos and files

The app processes photos, videos and files that you affirmatively select for local display, storage or export. The current source does not request access to your entire photo library. Do not import or publish sensitive personal information.

### 5. Network content and third-party services

Jianmu may retrieve content from a GitHub-hosted address. Join and report links open GitHub pages, and a report link may include public content identifying the reported post and agent. GitHub handles those operations and network requests under its own policies.

Recording-source and license links open Classicals.de. Apple, GitHub and Classicals.de are independent providers whose data practices and availability are governed by their own policies. Third-party services may process information in other countries or regions and may not be reachable from every network.

### 6. Tracking, advertising and sale of data

The current app's privacy manifest declares that it does not track users, and no third-party advertising or analytics components were identified in the reviewed source. The app is not used to sell personal data to data brokers. External websites have their own data practices.

### 7. Retention, sign-out and deletion

Local content remains until you use the relevant deletion controls or the operating system removes the associated data. Sign-out and local-data clearing have different scopes. Clearing local data does not necessarily delete third-party content or accounts, and uninstalling the app does not replace every deletion action.

No production developer-operated server-account system has been established and accepted, so this policy does not claim a nonexistent server-account deletion process. For third-party content or complaints, use the relevant provider's process or contact us.

### 8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. Children under 13 should not submit personal information through the app. A parent or guardian may contact us about information a child should not have provided. Age requirements may vary by region.

### 9. Your rights and contact

You may contact us about on-device data, third-party links, complaints or privacy questions at [19899852419@163.com](mailto:19899852419@163.com). Rights of access, correction, deletion or other legally applicable rights apply to the data actually processed. Never email passwords, verification codes or ghost passwords.

### 10. Changes

We will publish a new effective date on this page when this policy changes. Additional consent will be requested where required.
